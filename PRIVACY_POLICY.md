# Walk Intent Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 18, 2026**

ohesoft ("Developer") establishes this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of information in Walk Intent ("the App").

## 1. About Walk Intent

Walk Intent is a general wellness and walking habit app designed to help users build a walking routine by choosing a purpose, tracking walking time, keeping short notes, and maintaining streaks.

The App does not require account registration or login.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally on your device in order to provide its features:

- Walk session start and end times
- Walk duration
- Selected walking purpose, such as Music, Explore, Shopping, Errand, Think, or custom purposes
- Optional notes entered by the user
- Daily walking goal
- Streak information
- Walk history
- Notification settings
- Theme and language settings
- Other application preferences

This information is primarily stored and processed locally on the user's device.

The Developer does not require users to provide their name, postal address, phone number, date of birth, or other directly identifying personal information in order to use the App.

## 3. Notes Entered by Users

Users may optionally enter notes about their walks.

These notes are intended to be stored locally on the device.

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information in notes unless they are comfortable storing such information on their own device.

## 4. Location, Steps, and Health Data

The App does not currently collect or use:

- GPS location
- Walking routes
- Precise location data
- Step count
- Distance traveled
- Calorie data
- Apple Health / HealthKit data
- Health Connect data
- Google Fit data
- Apple Watch health data
- Wear OS health data

The App tracks walking activity based primarily on user-started walk timers and locally stored session information.

If these features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before such data is collected or processed.

## 5. Firebase Analytics

The App may use Firebase Analytics, provided by Google LLC, to understand general app usage and improve the App.

Firebase Analytics may process information such as:

- App version
- Device type
- Operating system and version
- App usage events
- Session information
- General technical information
- IP address or related network information as processed by Google
- Other information necessary for Firebase Analytics to operate

This information may be used to understand how features are used, improve usability, and improve the overall quality of the App.

The Developer does not use Firebase Analytics for third-party advertising or behavioral advertising.

## 6. Firebase Crashlytics

The App may use Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google LLC, to identify crashes, errors, and technical problems.

Crashlytics may process information such as:

- Crash reports
- Error logs
- Device model
- Operating system version
- App version
- Technical identifiers
- Application state at the time of a crash
- Other diagnostic information necessary to investigate software errors

This information is used only for debugging, stability improvements, and application quality improvement.

## 7. In-App Purchases and RevenueCat

The App may offer optional Pro features as a one-time in-app purchase.

Payments are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play.

The App may use RevenueCat, provided by RevenueCat, Inc., to manage purchase status, restore purchases, and determine whether Pro features should be enabled.

RevenueCat may process information such as:

- Product purchased
- Purchase status
- Purchase and restoration information
- Store transaction information
- App-related identifiers
- Device and application information necessary to provide purchase management services

The Developer does not directly receive or store complete payment information such as:

- Credit card numbers
- Debit card numbers
- Bank account information

Payment processing is handled by Apple or Google.

## 8. Notifications

The App may use local notifications for features such as:

- Daily walking goal reminders
- Goal completion notifications
- Scheduled walking reminders

Notifications are only used when permitted by the user.

Notification permissions can be changed at any time through the App or device settings.

## 9. Advertising

The App does not display third-party advertisements.

The App does not use advertising SDKs for personalized advertising.

The Developer does not sell user information for advertising purposes.

## 10. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

- Google Firebase Analytics
- Google Firebase Crashlytics
- RevenueCat
- Apple App Store
- Google Play

Information handled by these services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

## 11. Purposes of Data Processing

Information processed by the App or related third-party services may be used for the following purposes:

- Providing core App functionality
- Saving walk sessions and settings
- Displaying history, statistics, and streaks
- Managing Pro purchase status
- Restoring purchases
- Sending requested notifications
- Understanding general App usage
- Investigating crashes and technical problems
- Improving App stability and usability
- Preventing misuse or fraud
- Complying with applicable laws and legal obligations

## 12. Sale of Personal Information

The Developer does not sell users' personal information.

The Developer does not provide personal information to third parties for targeted advertising purposes.

Third-party service providers such as Google, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google Play may process limited information as necessary to provide their respective services.

## 13. Data Retention

Walk history, purposes, notes, streaks, settings, and other App data are generally stored locally on the user's device until deleted by the user or removed through uninstallation of the App.

Information processed by Firebase, RevenueCat, Apple, or Google may be retained according to the retention policies of those service providers.

## 14. Data Deletion

Walk Intent does not require users to create a Developer-managed account.

Locally stored App data may be deleted using an in-app data reset function when available, or by uninstalling the App.

Data processed by third-party services may be subject to the deletion and retention policies of those service providers.

For questions regarding data handling, users may contact the Developer using the email address provided below.

## 15. Data Security

The Developer takes reasonable measures to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss of information handled by the App.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

## 16. Health and Medical Disclaimer

Walk Intent is a general wellness and habit-building application.

The App is not a medical device and is not intended to provide:

- Medical diagnosis
- Medical treatment
- Disease prevention
- Professional medical advice

Users should walk and exercise within their own physical capabilities.

Users with medical conditions, injuries, exercise restrictions, or other health concerns should consult an appropriate healthcare professional when necessary.

## 17. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

If applicable law requires parental consent or other protections for younger users, the Developer will take appropriate measures.

## 18. International Data Processing

Third-party services used by the App, including Google, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google Play, may process or store information outside the user's country or region.

Such processing is governed by the applicable laws and privacy policies of those service providers.

## 19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when:

- App features change
- New third-party services are introduced
- Data handling practices change
- Applicable laws or platform requirements change

The updated Privacy Policy will become effective when published.

Where appropriate, significant changes may also be communicated within the App.
